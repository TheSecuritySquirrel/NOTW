The RAM Shortage Could Last Years

Read Full Story
  • RAM supply is projected to meet only about 60% of demand by 2027 — a gap that shows no sign of closing quickly.
  • AI infrastructure build-out is the primary driver, with data centers consuming memory at unprecedented scale.
  • Prices are expected to keep rising as high demand collides with a supply chain that can't keep pace.
Cybersecurity & Policy

NIST to Stop Rating Non-Priority Flaws Due to Volume Increases

  • CVE vulnerability submissions have surged 263% since 2020, making it impossible for NIST to analyze every reported flaw.
  • Going forward, NIST will only score high-priority vulnerabilities — those that are actively exploited or affect critical systems.
  • Security teams may need to lean more heavily on vendor-provided data or alternative sources, increasing the risk of missed or poorly understood vulnerabilities.

Hackers Are Abusing Unpatched Windows Security Flaws to Break Into Organizations

  • Hackers are already exploiting recently disclosed Windows vulnerabilities to breach real organizations — with attacks moving fast.
  • Only one of the actively exploited bugs has been patched so far, leaving others as open doors for attackers.
