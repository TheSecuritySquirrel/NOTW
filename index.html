Hardware & Supply Chain 01 / 03

The RAM Shortage Could Last Years

Read full story ↗
Supply Gap
RAM supply is projected to cover only ~60% of demand by 2027 — meaning nearly half of what the market needs simply won't exist.
Root Cause
AI data center buildout is the primary driver, consuming memory at a pace that traditional manufacturing cannot match.
Impact on You
Expect continued price increases on consumer and enterprise hardware — budgets for PCs, servers, and upgrades will feel the squeeze.
Cybersecurity & Policy 02 / 03

NIST to Stop Rating Non-Priority Vulnerabilities

The Problem
CVE submissions have surged 263% since 2020. NIST is overwhelmed and can no longer score every reported vulnerability.
What Changes
NIST will now only analyze high-priority flaws — those actively exploited in the wild or affecting critical infrastructure.
Why It Matters
Security teams will be forced to rely more on vendor data and alternative sources — increasing the risk that dangerous vulnerabilities go unnoticed or misunderstood.
Active Threats 03 / 03

Hackers Are Exploiting Unpatched Windows Flaws Right Now

Active Exploitation
Attackers are already using recently disclosed Windows vulnerabilities to breach real organizations — this is not a theoretical threat.
Patch Status
Only one of the exploited flaws has been patched. The remaining vulnerabilities are live, unaddressed, and actively targeted.
Accelerant
A researcher's public release of exploit code gave attackers a ready-made weapon — dramatically shortening the window between disclosure and attack.
